# MASTERED LANGUAGE COACH
## Malayalam → English | Mission 01: My First Introduction

### Mission outcome

The learner can introduce themselves in English using their name, place, and one simple personal detail in 20–30 seconds.

### Learner-facing explanation

Today you will not study a large grammar lesson. You will speak a short English introduction. Your aim is clear speech and confidence, not perfection. Listen, repeat, and then say the introduction in your own words.

### Key English phrases

| English | Malayalam meaning | Pronunciation guide |
|---|---|---|
| Hello. | നമസ്കാരം | heh-LOH |
| My name is ___. | എന്റെ പേര് ___ ആണ്. | my NAYM iz ___ |
| I am from ___. | ഞാൻ ___ ൽ നിന്നാണ്. | eye am from ___ |
| I live in ___. | ഞാൻ ___ ൽ താമസിക്കുന്നു. | eye liv in ___ |
| I am a ___. | ഞാൻ ഒരു ___ ആണ്. | eye am a ___ |
| Nice to meet you. | നിങ്ങളെ കണ്ടതിൽ സന്തോഷം. | nys too meet yoo |

### One useful note

Use **My name is** to say your name. Use **I am from** for your hometown or country. Do not worry about a long introduction yet.

### Model dialogue

**A:** Hello. My name is Anu. I am from Kerala. I live in Kozhikode. I am a student. Nice to meet you.

**B:** Hello, Anu. Nice to meet you too.

### Video script brief

Create a warm 60–90 second beginner lesson for Malayalam speakers. Explain the six phrases above in Malayalam, show each English phrase clearly, give the pronunciation guide, and model the dialogue. End with: “Now introduce yourself in English.” Keep the language encouraging and natural.

### Audio practice brief

Create a 2–4 minute listen-and-repeat practice. Say each English phrase once slowly, pause five seconds for the learner to repeat, then say it once at natural speed. End with a 20-second guided self-introduction using blanks for name, place, city, and role.

### PDF lesson and practice sheet

Include: mission goal; key phrase table; a short pronunciation practice; model dialogue; fill-in-the-blank self-introduction; a record-yourself task; and this self-check:

- I said hello.
- I said my name.
- I said where I am from.
- I said where I live or what I do.
- I finished with “Nice to meet you.”

### AI role-play prompt

You are a friendly English conversation partner for a beginner Malayalam speaker. Start in English with: “Hello! Nice to meet you. Please introduce yourself.” Let the learner respond. Give short, kind corrections in Malayalam for fluency, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, response quality, and confidence. Ask the learner to retry if they do not give at least three details: name, place, and one personal detail. Keep the English simple and do not overload the learner.

### Completion rule

The learner completes the mission when they give a 20–30 second introduction with at least three correct personal details and can respond politely to “Nice to meet you.”
